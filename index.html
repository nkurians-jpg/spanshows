Who We Are

A Bridge Between Two Homes

SPAN Entertainment Inc. exists for one reason — to close the distance. We bring the artists you grew up loving from Kerala and across India to the cities you now call home in Canada.

From silver-screen icons and chart-topping musicians to the new wave of Malayalam rap and hip-hop, we curate premium live experiences that feel like a celebration of culture, language, and belonging.

Every show is produced end-to-end with care — venues, sound, hospitality, and an audience that feels like family.
